# People can’t hear me in Teams

For a Windows laptop using the Teams desktop app and a meeting-room microphone.

1. Check the **Mute** button in the call and the physical mute button on the speakerphone.
2. In Teams, open **Settings and more (…) → Settings → Devices**. Under **Microphone**, choose the room speakerphone by name.
3. Say a short sentence and ask someone at the other end to repeat it. If they can hear you clearly, you can continue the meeting.
4. If they still hear silence, tell the service desk the room, microphone name, call time and whether Windows shows input when you speak under **Settings → System → Sound → Input**.
